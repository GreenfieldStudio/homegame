Privacy Policy

Home Game stores all your data locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage. We have zero access to your information.

Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server
  • No user accounts or login credentials exist
  • No cookies are used
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts are loaded
  • No data ever leaves your device
  • No advertisements are served

If you clear your browser's data, your game history will be erased because it only exists on your device. We recommend using the data export feature to keep backups.

What Home Game Does

Home Game helps poker night hosts track the numbers so nobody has to do math on napkins.

  • Track buy-ins, rebuys, and cash-outs for each player during a session
  • Calculate optimized end-of-night settlements (fewest payments needed)
  • Maintain lifetime leaderboards and player statistics
  • Show detailed player profiles with session history, streaks, and head-to-head records
  • Copy settlement summaries to share with the group
💾

Your Data

Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Your data stays on the device where you use the app
  • Clearing browser data or using private/incognito mode will erase your history
  • Data does not sync between devices
  • We cannot recover lost data because we never had it

We strongly recommend regularly exporting your data as a backup.
